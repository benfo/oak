# Never render an unbounded list in a row, menu or tooltip

Why: Sidebar shows the newest 100 tags plus "Go to tag..."; a commit row at most 6 badges and "+N" (checked-out ref stays); menus at most 12 refs per list with a disabled "N more" row.
