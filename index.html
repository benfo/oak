to see what’s changed.

Keep your attention on your work, with clear views and tools that appear where you need them.

01 / HISTORY

Find your way through the branches.

Follow the commit graph, jump to your current or recent branches, and filter the rest. Keep the branch you’re working on in focus.

02 / COMPARE

See the change you’re about to make.

Compare two commits, or compare your feature branch from its common ancestor with a target branch. Review the changes you would see in a pull request.

03 / COMMIT

Every line belongs where you intend.

Review inline or side by side, with syntax and word-level highlighting. Stage a file, a hunk or individual lines to build a focused commit.
